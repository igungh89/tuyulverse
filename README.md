# TuyulVerse 🧠

### An AI-Powered Business Ecosystem

**Building intelligent businesses at the intersection of AI, creativity, and automation.**

TuyulVerse is an AI-powered business ecosystem created by **Lavanya Creative Studio (LCS)**, a creative and technology company driven by the vision of *Building What's Next.*

We explore how artificial intelligence, automation, and human creativity can work together to build innovative digital businesses, products, and experiences. TuyulVerse brings together independent ventures, each with its own identity and operating direction.
